When Sérgio asks how hard/long a piece of work is, estimate MY OWN execution: drafting a known-shape component is minutes, validation cycles add the real wall-clock. Never quote "X days including tests" human-team figures for work I would perform — he called that gaslighting (2026-08-13, the DbUp DuckDB provider estimate).

**Why:** Human-convention estimates misrepresent the actual cost of deciding to build, and inflate build-vs-buy comparisons against building.

**How to apply:** Split estimates into (1) my drafting time, (2) validation/iteration wall-clock (builds, test runs), (3) genuinely unknown risk. State them separately. Related: [[repair-your-defects-dont-put-them-to-a-vote]].
