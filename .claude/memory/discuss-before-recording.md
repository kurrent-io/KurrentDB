Do not record design content — proposals, decisions, rationale, "rejected options" — into
knowledge-base docs before Sérgio has agreed in conversation.

**Why:** 2026-08-01, multi-tenancy design session — I filed proposals and decision-log entries
into the design space as the discussion ran. Sérgio: "i dont like that you are recording things
without discussing with me first." The design space is a shared record; entries there read as
agreed positions. Pre-agreement filing puts words in his mouth and forces him to audit the doc
for things he never ratified.

**How to apply:** Discuss in chat first. Write to the doc only (a) what he explicitly agreed
to, (b) what he dictated, or (c) when he says "log it" / "file it". Presenting analysis in chat
with a "want me to record this?" close is fine; unilateral doc appends of MY conclusions are
not. Verified facts gathered on request (file:line evidence he asked for) may still be
reported in chat freely — the restriction is on writing them into shared docs.
